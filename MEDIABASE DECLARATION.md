# Mediabase — Declaration

This document defines what Mediabase is and why it exists. It is the
reference for project purpose and business model — see `PLANNING.md` for
infrastructure and `STACK.md` for technology.

## Vision

Mediabase is a digital media platform for publishing, distributing, and
consuming audio and video. Anyone can publish; consumers pay a fixed monthly
subscription for access to all content. The platform is built to maximize
the share of revenue that reaches creators, not shareholders.

## Ownership: Stiftelse (Foundation)

Mediabase is owned and operated by a Swedish stiftelse, not a company. The
foundation:

- Develops and maintains the platform.
- Can employ staff, sell subscriptions, and operate commercially.
- Has no owners and distributes no profit to any.

Surplus is used, in order of priority: maintain and improve the platform,
ensure long-term financial stability, increase payments to creators.

## Purpose (Ändamål)

**Swedish:** Stiftelsens ändamål är att främja digitalt skapande och
tillgängliggörande av media genom en öppen plattform där innehållsskapare kan
publicera, distribuera och erhålla ersättning för sitt innehåll.

**English:** The purpose of the Foundation is to promote the creation and
accessibility of digital media through an open platform where content
creators can publish, distribute, and receive compensation for their work.

Any surplus beyond what operating and developing the platform requires
benefits creators rather than accumulating as profit.

## Business Model

Subscription-based. Example at €10/month:

- The foundation retains a small operating fee (~1%, subject to change).
- The remainder is distributed directly to creators.

As the platform becomes more efficient to run, the fee should shrink and the
creator share should grow.

## Creator Compensation

Payment is based on actual consumption, not ads or algorithmic weighting.
Each user's subscription forms a personal payment pool, split among creators
in proportion to that user's viewing/listening time.

Example: a user pays €10/month and splits their time 50% Creator A / 30%
Creator B / 20% Creator C. After the platform fee, their payment is divided
in those proportions.

This rewards genuine engagement over clicks or ad impressions.

## Core Principles

- Creator-first economics.
- Transparency in how money is distributed.
- No pressure to maximize shareholder returns.
- Long-term sustainability over short-term profit.
- Open publishing model.
- Fair compensation based on real audience engagement.
- Low platform fees.
- Continuous reinvestment into the service.

## Overall Concept

Mediabase is infrastructure, not a profit-extracting intermediary. Ownership
by a foundation and revenue distribution tied to real consumption are meant
to make it a transparent, sustainable, creator-focused alternative to
conventional media platforms.
